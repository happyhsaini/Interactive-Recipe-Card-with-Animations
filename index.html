Velvet Chocolate Cake

What you need

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 1/2 cups caster sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup chocolate ganache
Method

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease an 8-inch cake tin.
  2. Whisk flour, cocoa, sugar, and baking powder until evenly mixed.
  3. Add eggs, warm milk, butter, and vanilla, then beat into a smooth batter.
  4. Pour into the tin and bake for 30 minutes, until the center springs back.
  5. Cool the cake fully before spreading ganache over the top.
  6. Slice, garnish with berries, and serve while the ganache is glossy.
